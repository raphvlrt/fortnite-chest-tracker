# Fortnite Chest Tracker

By TikTok [@raphvlrt](https://www.tiktok.com/@raphvlrt) · Discord: https://discord.gg/Cmf4tbV7mt

Windows app that counts the chests you open in every Fortnite game, with an overlay for your stream, your game history, a leaderboard, a rewards checklist and the drop odds. In English, French, Spanish, Chinese and Arabic.

**[⬇️ Download the latest version](https://github.com/raphvlrt/fortnite-chest-tracker/releases/latest/download/Fortnite-Chest-Tracker-Setup-1.0.5.exe)** (Windows installer, 118 MB). The app then updates itself.

**🌐 Website: [chest-tracker.pages.dev](https://chest-tracker.pages.dev)**, open to everyone. Sign in with Discord or Google in the app (Settings › Account) to see your stats on your phone or any computer, or count your chests by hand on console and mobile.

## How it works

At the end of each game, the app reads the replay Fortnite saves on your PC and counts the chests you opened. It does not modify the game and does not read its memory.

In Fortnite:
- turn on replays (Settings › Game › Replays);
- play in Windowed Fullscreen to see the overlay.

## Leaderboard

Players are ranked by the chests the app counted from their replays (this week, this season or all time). Chests added by hand and stats from before never count. You are on it by default under your Epic name; untick **Show me** in the Leaderboard tab to leave at any time.
