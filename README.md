# Fortnite Chest Tracker

By TikTok [@raphvlrt](https://www.tiktok.com/@raphvlrt) · Discord: https://discord.gg/Cmf4tbV7mt

Windows app that counts the chests you open in every Fortnite game, with an overlay for your stream, your game history, a rewards checklist and the drop odds. In English and French.

**[⬇️ Download the latest version](https://github.com/raphvlrt/fortnite-chest-tracker/releases/latest/download/Fortnite-Chest-Tracker-Setup-1.0.0.exe)** (Windows installer, 118 MB). The app then updates itself.

## How it works

At the end of each game, the app reads the replay Fortnite saves on your PC and counts the chests you opened. It does not modify the game and does not read its memory.

In Fortnite:
- turn on replays (Settings › Game › Replays);
- play in Windowed Fullscreen to see the overlay.
